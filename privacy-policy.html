1. Who we are

This extension is operated by Omoxo Fashion Designs ("we", "us"). Contact: info@omoxofashion.co.in · Website: https://omoxofashion.co.in

2. What the extension does

When you search on Google (google.com or google.co.in), the extension may show one clearly labelled "Sponsored" product suggestion from [Your Brand] above the search results. This is the extension's single purpose.

3. What data the extension handles

The extension reads only the search text (the q parameter in the address bar) of Google search result pages. It does not read the content of the search results, other websites, your browsing history, cookies, passwords, or any account information.

Filtering happens inside your browser first. The search text is sent to our server only if it contains one of our product keywords and none of our blocked ("negative") keywords. Searches that do not match are never sent anywhere.

When a search is sent, the data is:

  • the search text (limited to a maximum length), and
  • standard technical information that any web request includes, such as your IP address and timestamp.

We do not collect your name, email address, location, or any account identifier through this extension.

4. How we use the data

The search text is used only to decide which product suggestion (if any) to show you for that search. We do not:

  • sell or rent your data;
  • use it to build advertising profiles or to show you ads elsewhere;
  • use or transfer it for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose;
  • use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

5. Storage and retention

Our server processes the search text in memory to find a match and does not store it in a database or log it. The extension keeps recent results in temporary memory only while the browser is running; nothing is saved to disk.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server/access logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and operations, under its own policies.

6. Third-party service providers

We use the following providers only to operate the service:

  • Render (render.com) - hosts our server that receives the search text.
  • Hugging Face (huggingface.co) - if no direct keyword match is found, the search text may be sent to Hugging Face's inference service solely to compute a text-similarity score. It is not sent for any other purpose.

[Edit this list if you change providers.] We do not share data with anyone else, except where required by law.

7. Security

Data sent from the extension to our server is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

8. Your choices

  • You can disable or uninstall the extension at any time from chrome://extensions; it will then stop reading or sending anything.
  • You can contact us to ask questions or request deletion of any data relating to you.
